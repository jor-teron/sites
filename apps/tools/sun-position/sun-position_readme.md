# Sun Position (SP1)

Offline sun-position app. No libraries, no network. It opens on Diphu, then you can use this device's location, pick a city, or type coordinates.

- Files: `sun-position.html`, `sun-position.css`, `sun-position.js` (RELEASE = 'SP1', `?v=SP1`), `sp_calc.js` (NOAA solar maths, node-testable), `sp_cities.js` (Indian state and UT capitals plus major world cities, each with an IANA time zone), `index.html` redirect, `sun-position_icon.svg`, `test_sp_calc.js` (`node test_sp_calc.js`).
- Storage: localStorage `sun-position-v1` = `{theme:'auto'|'light'|'dark', tzMode:'ist'|'place', places:[{id,name,lat,lon,tz}], currentPlace, askedPlace}`. Auto theme follows `sites-hub.theme` (else the device setting). Saved places and the current place stay on the device. Add or remove them with in-app dialogs (no `alert` / `confirm`).
- Location: `navigator.geolocation` (used only when you ask), the built-in city list, or typed latitude and longitude. GPS and custom places store a time zone (the device zone, IST, or one you pick).
- Times: IST is the default. “This place” uses the city's IANA zone, or the zone saved with a custom / GPS place. A date picker and a time slider show the sun at any hour; Now returns to a live view (refreshed every 15 seconds, not a render loop).
- Dial: a compass with N at the top, today's above-horizon path as an arc, and the sun at its azimuth and height (centre is straight up, the rim is the horizon). Direction is degrees clockwise from north. Height is degrees above or below the horizon, including NOAA refraction.
- Times list: sunrise, sunset, solar noon, day length, first and last light (civil twilight), nautical twilight is computed but not listed, golden hour (elevation −4° to 6°) and blue hour (−6° to −4°). Polar day and night are named plainly when the sun does not set or rise.
- Maths: NOAA Solar Calculator (Meeus) in `sp_calc.js`. Official sunrise and sunset use a 90.833° zenith. Checked against known values for Delhi, Guwahati, London and Tromsø, plus the Golden, Colorado 2003-10-17 NOAA/NREL example.
- Limits: times are usually within one or two minutes of an almanac; the NOAA method is less accurate above about 72° latitude. Refraction is an average, not adjusted for temperature or pressure. The city list is a small offline table, not a complete gazetteer. A custom place does not guess its time zone from the map; you pick one.
