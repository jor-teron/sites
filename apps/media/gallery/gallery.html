About

Gallery release GA2

Browse photos and videos from a folder or files on this device. Files are read locally and never uploaded; only settings (and, where the browser supports it, the last folder's permission handle) are stored.

Viewer: swipe or ←/→, pinch / double-tap / wheel to zoom, drag to pan, Esc to close.

Videos: Space play/pause, ←/→ seek ±10 s (Shift+←/→ or PgUp/PgDn for previous/next item), F fullscreen, M mute, double-tap left/right to skip. A same-name .srt/.vtt in the folder loads as subtitles; playback position is remembered.
