    About

    Hub Photo Studio

    The Hub's bigger photo editor. Layers (images, text and shapes, with opacity and blend modes), selections (rectangle, ellipse, lasso, magic wand) and layer masks, crop, rotate, flip, resize (smaller or larger), brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth, sharpen and looks, with undo and redo. Edits stay as settings on top of your original photo and are only applied when you save, print or send. Save your work as a Photo Studio project (.hps) to keep editing later, or export the layers as .ora for GIMP and Krita. Everything runs on this device; nothing is uploaded.

    Built on Photo Editor v2. Retouch and pro adjustments are planned.
