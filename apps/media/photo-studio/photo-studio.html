About

Hub Photo Studio

The Hub's bigger photo editor. Crop, rotate, flip, resize (smaller or larger), brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth, sharpen and looks, with undo and redo. Edits stay as settings on top of your original photo and are only applied when you save, print or send. Everything runs on this device; nothing is uploaded.

Built on Photo Editor v2. Layers, selections, retouch and pro adjustments are planned.
