GIF Maker release GM2

Turns a short video — or a set of photos — into an animated GIF, entirely on your device — nothing is uploaded. Pick a range of up to 15 s, a width, frame rate and palette, then Make GIF.

Photos: add several images, reorder by dragging (long-press on touch) or ◀ ▶, set a delay per frame.

Encoding: gifenc by Matt DesLauriers (MIT).

Swipe left/right or use ←/→ to switch screens.
