    How to play

    Roll the die and follow the numbered path from 1 to 100. Land at a ladder's foot to climb. Land on a snake's head to slide down its body. Only one connector is used each turn.

    Your table. Your rules.

    • Exact 100 (on by default): an overshoot stays put. With it off you stop at 100.
    • Six rolls again: enjoy another turn after any six.
    • Six to start: wait at the gate until you roll six. That roll enters at square 1.

    Play together or with computers

    Choose 2–4 active seats. Human seats take turns on this device; CPU seats roll automatically. This is a game of chance: Easy / Medium / Hard change CPU pace and coaching detail only. Everyone uses the same fair six-sided die.

    Use the Roll button (or Space when the board has focus). Progress saves after each complete turn. Quit or reload during a roll returns to the previous complete turn. Resume is on the welcome screen. CPU players wait while the tab is hidden or help is open.

    System reduced-motion always takes priority. This switch uses shared Game Settings → Low graphics. The gear opens theme (Auto / Light / Dark) and volume; Auto follows the Hub, then your device.
