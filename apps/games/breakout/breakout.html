How to play

Clear every coloured brick. Grey crossed bricks cannot break and do not count toward clearing a level. Dots show hits remaining.

Move with the mouse, drag on the board, or use ← → / A D. Tap Launch or press Space. A hit near a paddle edge sends the ball sideways; the centre sends it up. Speed increases as you play.

Catch falling capsules: W wider paddle · M multi-ball · S slow ball · L automatic lasers · + extra life (up to 9). Timed powers last 16 seconds and can be refreshed.

You start with 3 lives. Lose a life only when the last ball falls. Powers end on life loss or a new level. Brick hits earn 10 points and breaking one adds 40. Clearing a level adds a bonus.

Ten handmade boards lead to endless random levels. Best score and progress save automatically. P / Esc pauses; switching tabs always pauses. Shared Settings controls theme and sound.
