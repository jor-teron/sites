How to play

Swap two neighbouring candies by tapping them or swiping. Mouse dragging works too. A swap must make a line of at least 3 or it returns without using a move.

Matches clear and candies drop. Each cascade adds a multiplier: ×1 then ×2 then ×3 and so on. Each cleared candy scores 60 × the multiplier.

You have 25 moves. Reach the target for one star; keep playing for two or three! Shapes distinguish all six colours. A glow suggests a move after 5 seconds idle. Stuck boards reshuffle for free.

Keyboard: Tab or arrows to a candy then Enter/Space to select; select a neighbour to swap. Escape pauses. Ten generated score challenges; special candies come later.
