How to play

This is a living ant farm. Workers dig tunnels, bring food home, and raise brood while the queen lays eggs.

  • Food and Water drop from the sky where you tap. Water soaks the soil (it dries later). Food is also found naturally by day.
  • Dig to carve a cell of soil next to a tunnel or the surface.
  • Look, or tap an ant or a chamber, to inspect it.
  • Drag to pan, pinch or use the wheel to zoom. 0 fills the view, F fits the whole farm.
  • At night the foragers sleep in the nest. Open the gear for theme, mute, and low graphics.
