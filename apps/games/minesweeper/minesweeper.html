How to play

Reveal every safe cell without hitting a mine. Numbers count mines in the eight surrounding cells.

Your first reveal opens an area: that cell and all its neighbours contain no mines.

Right-click or hold a cell for 400 ms to flag it. On phones enable Flag mode to flag with a tap. Optional ? marks cycle after a flag.

Click an open number to chord: when its adjacent flag count matches its number it reveals the remaining neighbours. Wrong flags can explode a mine!

Arrows move keyboard focus; Enter or Space reveals; F flags. Drag to pan Hard or choose larger cells. Browser zoom is not disabled.

Custom sizes clamp to 5–40 columns and 5–30 rows. Mines clamp to 1 through the number of cells minus 9 to reserve a safe opening.

Games save after each move. The timer pauses when hidden or help is open. Best times are kept by difficulty and separately for each custom size.

Smiley or a new difficulty starts over. A safe opening does not guarantee every board can be solved without guessing.
