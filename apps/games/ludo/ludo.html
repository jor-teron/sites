Ludo

Pick the players

New game: who plays?

Tap the colours that play (2 to 4). Two players sit opposite each other. Tap the button beside a colour to make it a person or the computer (Easy, Medium or Hard).

House rules

How to play

  • The aim: get all 4 of your tokens once round the board and into the middle before anyone else.
  • Your turn: tap the die. The tokens you can move light up, and a dot shows where each one would land. Tap the one you want.
  • Starting: tokens wait in your corner. You need a 6 to bring one out onto your coloured start square.
  • Rolling a 6: you get another go. But if you roll three 6s in a row, you lose your turn.
  • Knocking out: land on a single token of another colour and it goes back to its corner. You get another go too.
  • Safe squares: squares with a star, and every coloured start square, are safe. Nobody can be knocked out there, and tokens can share them.
  • Going home: after one lap your token turns up the path of your own colour to the middle. You need the exact number to get in, and getting a token home gives you another go.
  • Stuck? If none of your tokens can move, your turn is skipped for you.
  • Winning: the first player with all 4 tokens home wins. Everyone else plays on for 2nd, 3rd and 4th.
  • Computer players: in New game, tap the button next to a colour to make it the computer (Easy, Medium or Hard). It plays by itself, a little slowly, so you can follow.
  • Auto-move: when there is only one move you can make, it is made for you.
  • House rules: tick them in New game to change how you play: no exact roll to finish, a 1 or a 6 to come out, no extra go for knocking out, or blocks (two of your tokens on one square stop everyone else getting past).
