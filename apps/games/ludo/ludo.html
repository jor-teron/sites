Ludo

Pick the players

New game: who plays?

Tap the colours that play (2 to 4). Two players sit opposite each other. Tap the button beside a colour to make it a person or the computer (Easy, Medium or Hard).

How to play

  • Goal: be the first to race all 4 of your tokens round the board and into the centre.
  • Roll: tap the die. Tokens that can move glow, and a dot shows where each would land. Tap one to move it.
  • Coming out: a token leaves its yard only on a 6, onto your coloured start square.
  • 6s: a 6 gives you another roll. Three 6s in a row and you lose the turn.
  • Capture: land on a single rival token to send it back to its yard, and roll again. Squares with a star, and every start square, are safe: no captures there, and tokens can share them.
  • Home: after one lap, go up your coloured column to the centre. You need the exact number to finish. Getting a token home gives you another roll.
  • If you can't move, the turn passes by itself. The others play on for 2nd, 3rd and 4th place.
  • Computer players: in New game, set any colour to the computer at Easy, Medium or Hard. It rolls and moves by itself with a short pause so you can follow.
  • Auto-move: when only one move is possible it is made for you.
