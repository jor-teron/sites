How to play

  • Each side has 2 pawns. They start on their own home squares (the ringed squares in their colour).
  • Goal: be the first to get one pawn onto either of the other side's home squares.
  • Move: tap one of your pawns, then tap a dot. A pawn goes 1 or 2 squares straight (up, down, left or right), or 1 square diagonally. It can hop over a pawn but never through a wall.
  • Wall: after moving you may place one wall. Tap a groove between squares to see it, Rotate to switch green (up-down) and blue (side to side), then tap again or press Place wall. Or press Skip wall.
  • Each side has 9 green and 9 blue walls. Walls are 2 squares long and can't overlap or cross.
  • You may slow a pawn down, but never shut it in: every pawn must always keep a way to the other side's home.
