How to play

Drag a piece with your mouse or finger. Bring it near its place on the board or near a matching neighbour to snap. Joined pieces move as one group. Pieces on the board stay put.

Edges only hides loose groups without border pieces. Ghost preview shows a faint picture on the board. Pieces never rotate in this release.

The timer starts with your first drag. Pause or leave the tab to take a break. Your latest puzzle and best times are saved automatically on this device.

Six original pictures are built in. Your own photo is processed here with no upload. Large photos may be available only until you close this tab.
