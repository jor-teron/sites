How to play

  • Black and White take turns putting one stone on a crossing of the lines. Black goes first.
  • The empty crossings next to a stone (or a joined group) are its liberties.
  • Fill a group's last liberty and you capture it: those stones come off the board.
  • You can't play where your stone would have no liberties, unless that move captures.
  • Ko: if one stone was just taken, you can't take one straight back. Play elsewhere first.
  • Nothing useful left? Press Pass. Two passes in a row end the game.
  • Then tap groups that are surely lost (dead) and press Count score.
  • Score = your stones + the empty points only you surround. White gets 6.5 extra (komi) for going second.
  • Small screen? Set Tap twice: the first tap shows the stone, the second places it.
