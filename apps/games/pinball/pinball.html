How to play

  • Keep the ball on the table with the two flippers. You have 3 balls.
  • Launch: hold Space (or hold the bottom-right corner of the screen), then let go. Hold longer for a stronger shot.
  • Flippers: Z or ← for the left one, / or → for the right one. On a phone, hold the left or right half of the screen.
  • Star lanes: roll through the three stars at the top to light them; each flipper press moves the lit stars one step. All three lit: +2,000 and the bonus multiplier goes up (up to ×5).
  • Missions: sink the ball in the hole under the stars to start one (the panel shows it and your progress). Finish it to score and rise in rank, from Cadet to Admiral.
  • Ramp: shoot up the covered entry on the right; a strong shot rides over the top and comes back to the left flipper. The spinner is in the lane beside it. Knock down all four drop targets on the left to clear them.
  • Bonus: builds up as you play and is paid × the multiplier when the ball drains. Ball save: lose a new ball in the first few seconds and you get it back.
  • A ball that falls between or beside the flippers is lost. A game in progress is kept if you leave, and your best score is kept.
