How to play

Keep the ball on the table with the two flippers. You have 3 balls. A ball that falls between or beside the flippers is lost.

Controls

Hold longer to launch harder. Nudge gives the ball a small push left, right or up. Nudge too often (3 times in a few seconds) and you get a tilt warning; 5 times and the table tilts: the flippers stop until that ball drains and its bonus is lost.

Scoring

  • Star lanes at the top light up when the ball rolls through. Each flipper press moves the lit stars one step. All three lit: +2,000 and the bonus multiplier goes up (up to ×5).
  • Ramp: shoot up the leaning entry on the right. A strong shot rides over the top and comes back to the left flipper. Easiest from the left flipper: catch the ball on it (hold the flipper up), let go so it rolls, then flip.
  • Spinner in the right lane, pop bumpers, slingshots, and four drop targets on the left (knock all four down to clear them).
  • Bonus builds up as you play and is paid × the multiplier when the ball drains. Ball save: lose a new ball in the first 6 seconds and you get it back once.

Missions

Sink the ball in the hole under the stars to start the next mission. The panel shows it and your progress. Goals get bigger each time round the list.
