      How to play

      • Each side hides 5 ships on its own 10 x 10 grid: Carrier 5, Battleship 4, Cruiser 3, Submarine 3 and Destroyer 2.
      • Placing: drag a ship from the tray (or a ship already on the grid) onto a square. A green ghost fits, a red one does not. Picking a ship up lifts it off the board. Tap a square to drop the selected ship with its front end there — it will not slide if it hangs off the edge. Rotate turns it (across or down). Random places the whole fleet for you. Press Ready when every ship is down.
      • Firing: take turns. Tap a square on the big grid (the other side's waters) to fire one shot. A ringed dot is a miss; a mark on a ship is a hit. When every square of a ship is hit, it is sunk and listed as sunk.
      • The small grid is your own fleet, with the other side's shots on it. The lists under the grids show which ships are still afloat.
      • Goal: sink the other side's whole fleet first.
      • 2 players: hand the device over when asked. The screen is covered between turns so nobody sees the other fleet.
