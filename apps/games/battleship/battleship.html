      How to play

      • Each side hides a fleet on its own 10 x 10 grid. The classic fleet is Carrier 5, Battleship 4, Cruiser 3, Submarine 3 and Destroyer 2. Skirmish is a shorter game with Battleship 4, Cruiser 3 and Destroyer 2.
      • Placing: drag a ship from the tray (or a ship already on the grid) onto a square. A green ghost fits, a red one does not. Picking a ship up lifts it off the board. Tap a square to drop the selected ship with its front end there — it will not slide if it hangs off the edge. Rotate turns it (across or down). Random places the whole fleet for you. Press Ready when every ship is down. Ships may touch unless you tick Ships may not touch, which also forbids corner contact.
      • Firing: in Classic you fire one shot, then it is the other side's turn. In Salvo you fire one shot for every ship you still have afloat, counted at the start of your volley. Tap a square on the big grid (the other side's waters), or move with the arrow keys and press Enter or Space. A ringed dot is a miss; a mark on a ship is a hit. When every square of a ship is hit, it is sunk and listed as sunk.
      • The small grid is your own fleet, with the other side's shots on it. The lists under the grids show which ships are still afloat.
      • Goal: sink the other side's whole fleet first.
      • 2 players: hand the device over when asked. The screen is covered between turns so nobody sees the other fleet.
      • New game or a change of opponent, mode, fleet or the touching rule asks you to confirm if a match is already under way. Stats keeps games, wins, average shots, accuracy and streaks by mode and level. Older saved wins still show on the chips.
