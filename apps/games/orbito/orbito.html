How to play

  • Two players, 8 marbles each: Black and White. Black goes first. Against the computer you are Black.
  • Goal: get 4 of your marbles in a row: across, down or corner to corner.
  • Each turn has three steps:
  • 1. Push (if you like): tap one of your rival's marbles, then a green dot to move it one hole up, down, left or right into an empty hole.
  • 2. Place: tap an empty hole to put down one of your own marbles. You must do this.
  • 3. The board turns on its own: every marble on the outer ring moves one hole anticlockwise, and so do the 4 in the middle (follow the arrows).
  • Rows are checked only after the board turns. If both players have 4 in a row at once, it's a draw.
  • All 16 marbles down and no winner? The board turns up to 5 more times, checking each time. Still nobody: a draw.
  • Changed your mind about the push? Press Undo before you place. Undo after that takes back a whole turn (against the computer, its turn and yours).
