Vision release VS2

Cloud Vision (default when a key exists): labels, text (OCR), objects, landmarks & logos, colours and safe-search from Google Cloud Vision. The photo is sent to Google; about 1,000 units a month per feature are free. Key: the Hub's gvision (Settings → API Keys), or the field above when used on its own.

Ollama: ask a vision model on your own Ollama about a photo: describe it, read its text, turn it into an image-generation prompt, or ask anything — then keep asking follow-ups about the same photo. Photos are shrunk to about 1024 px before sending and kept on this device only.

Good models: llama3.2-vision, gemma3, qwen2.5vl, llava, minicpm-v. To let this site reach Ollama, start it with OLLAMA_ORIGINS=https://jor-teron.github.io.

Swipe left/right or use ←/→ to switch screens. Paste a photo with Ctrl/⌘+V.
