About Story

This webapp runs small language models entirely in your browser for generating short stories. Models are downloaded once and cached locally. Chat history is saved for 30 days. Models are refreshed every 3 months.

Lite: Tiny LilyStory (~0.5 MB) — instant start.
Mini: ~10 MB TinyStories model — better coherence.
Pro: ~49 MB IllaryiaStories — best quality among the three.

All processing is local. No data leaves your device after the initial download.
