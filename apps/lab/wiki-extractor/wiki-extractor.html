About

            
This tool extracts the main body text from English Wikipedia pages.

            
You enter a starting page link, choose how many levels deep to go, and set a page limit. It follows internal Wikipedia links and collects only the plain article text.

            
Sections such as See also, References, External links, Further reading, and Bibliography are automatically removed so you get clean main content.

            
Results can be viewed as a tree of children or as a flat list, then copied or downloaded as a text file.
