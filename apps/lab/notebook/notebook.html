About

Paper Notebook — a 50-page sketch pad for finger, mouse or pen. Everything is saved in this browser. Pages can be crumpled, never erased.

How to

  • Swipe ☰ left / right — previous / next page
  • Pull ☰ up — all pages grid
  • Pull ☰ down — crumple this page (+1 wrinkle level, max 10)
  • Hold ☰ — quick menu (PNG, PDF, reset)

Tips

  • Pull or swipe past a third of the screen — the jelly glows when it will fire.
  • Short pulls do nothing; play with the jelly.
  • Write your name on the cover label; fill the index on pages 2–3.
  • Crumples are permanent — no undo.
