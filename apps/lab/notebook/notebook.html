About

Paper Notebook — a 50-page sketch pad for finger, mouse or pen. Pages can be crumpled, never erased.

How to

  • swipe ☰ left / right → turn the page
  • pull ☰ up → all pages
  • pull ☰ down → crumple this page
  • hold ☰ → quick menu (PNG, this page)

Tips

  • pull past a third of the screen — the jelly glows when it will fire
  • short pulls just play with the jelly
  • write your name on the cover; fill the index on pages 2–3
  • crumples are forever ✿
