About

This is a lightweight, browser-based collection of games, tools, and media apps — all built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No installs, no accounts, no trackers.

It runs entirely in your browser, works offline once loaded, and is designed to be simple enough for kids and fast enough for everyone.

What's inside

  • Games Playable in the browser
  • Tools Calculator, Notepad, Weather, vDesktop
  • Media Gallery, Paint, Photo Editor, Webcam
  • TV Channels & kids' playlists

For kids & parents

The kids section is designed for ages 5–15. No ads, no tracking, no external accounts. Progress and scores are stored locally on your device using localStorage.

A phone can be used as a D-pad controller for tablet-based games through the Controller module.

Privacy

Nothing is uploaded. Nothing is shared. Everything stays on your device unless you explicitly export or share it.

Tech

  • Vanilla HTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • No build step, no framework
  • Works offline (static files)
  • Optional: WebSocket relay for phone-as-remote
