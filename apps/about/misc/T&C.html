Permission to Use This Network

1. Permission

Access to this network is openly provided by the network provider for public use. Users may connect freely through the WiFi portal, which serves as the formal point of consent and acknowledgement of the usage terms.

2. Why This Page is Required

Not every user can directly reach the network provider to get permission, nor is the provider always available to grant it. The portal streamlines authorization through a simple, standardized one-tap process — ensuring compliance with Indian IT regulations while maintaining an accessible and frictionless experience for all users.

By clicking the Log in button on the WiFi portal, you are requesting permission to use this network and acknowledging the usage terms.
