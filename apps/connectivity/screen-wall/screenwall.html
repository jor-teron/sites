    Multi-Screen Wall MW2

    Join phones, tablets and PCs on the same Hub Connect Code into one big screen. One device is the host: it arranges the others in a grid, sets the bezel gap and picks a test pattern, a big clock, scrolling text or a picture. Every display shows only its own slice, full screen, with the screen kept awake. Clocks and scrolling follow the host's time. Peer to peer over PeerJS; nothing is stored on a server. Video on the wall is planned for MW2.
