        About

        Telephone TP2b

        A phone for every device on the same Hub Connect Code. Each device gets its own 5-digit number (10000–99999), checked against the phones online before it is kept; if two devices still end up with the same number, the one that got it last picks a new one and says so. Dial it on the keypad or tap a phone in Online now: only that device rings, with Answer and Decline. Contacts keep names and numbers, show who is online, and ⭐ favourites. During a call: Mute, Speaker, Video (front or back camera) and Hang up. Recent calls keeps calls in, out and missed with a red badge for new missed calls; tap one to call back or ＋ to save it. Do Not Disturb declines calls silently (favourites can still ring). Calls ring only while Telephone is open on the other device. Peer to peer over PeerJS; nothing is recorded or stored on a server.
