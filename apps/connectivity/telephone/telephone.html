      Telephone TP1

      A phone for every device on the same Hub Connect Code. Each device gets its own 4-digit number. Dial it on the keypad or tap a phone in Online now: only that device rings, with Answer and Decline. During a call: Mute, Speaker and Hang up. Recent calls keeps calls in, out and missed; tap one to call back. Calls ring only while Telephone is open on the other device. Peer to peer over PeerJS; nothing is recorded or stored on a server.
