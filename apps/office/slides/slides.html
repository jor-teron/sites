    Keyboard

    Ctrl+Enter New slide after this one Tab / Shift+Tab Indent / outdent bullets Enter in the title: go to the bullets ↑ ↓ Previous / next slide (in the slide list) Alt+↑ ↓ Move the slide Ctrl+S Save now (✓ Saved) Ctrl+P Print (one 16:9 page per slide; also Save as PDF) Esc Close a menu or dialog

    Pictures, layouts, themes

    Ctrl+V Paste a picture (or drop one on the slide) Drag a picture to move it, a handle to resize it Shift while resizing: keep the shape ← → ↑ ↓ Nudge the picture (Shift: 10 px) Delete Delete the selected picture Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y Undo / redo pictures, layouts, themes (outside the text)

    Present

    F5 Present from the start Shift+F5 or P (outside the text) Present from this slide → ↓ Space PgDn Enter Next ← ↑ PgUp Backspace Previous Home / End First / last slide Number + Enter Go to that slide Tap right two-thirds / swipe left: next; tap left third / swipe right: previous Esc End the show (back on the slide shown last)

    About

    Hub Office Slides SL4

    Make simple slide decks: pick a layout for each slide, add titles, bullets and pictures, and give the deck a theme. Decks save on this device as you type, and you can keep several. Export a deck as a .hslides file (open JSON, pictures inside) to back it up or move it to another device. Press ▶ Present to show it full screen. Nothing is uploaded.

    SL4: the same ribbon and Quick Access bar (Undo, Redo, Save, Print) as Draftly and Spreadsheet, and Print / Export PDF with one 16:9 page per slide.
