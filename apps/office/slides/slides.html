    About

    Hub Office Slides SL10

    Make simple slide decks: pick a layout for each slide, add titles, bullets and pictures, and give the deck a theme. Decks save on this device as you type, and you can keep several. Export a deck as a .hslides file (open JSON, pictures inside) to back it up or move it to another device. Press ▶ Present to show it full screen. Nothing is uploaded.

    SL10: the editor starts even when the notes pane is restored before the canvas is ready. The ribbon, File menu, slide list and deck all load, and the slide is fitted once the canvas exists.

    SL9: export and import a PowerPoint (.pptx) file (best-effort), and export this slide or all slides as PNG (all in a zip). The .hslides format is unchanged.

    SL8: more deck themes (colours and fonts; the app itself stays Light / Dark), a footer with optional slide numbers and date, hide a slide from the show, and named sections in the slide list.
