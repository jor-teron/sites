    About

    Hub Office Slides SL2

    Make simple slide decks: a title and bullets on each slide. Decks save on this device as you type, and you can keep several. Export a deck as a .hslides file (open JSON) to back it up or move it to another device. Press ▶ Present to show it full screen. Nothing is uploaded.
