LOOKUP
  VLOOKUP(value, table, col, [approx])
  HLOOKUP(value, table, row, [approx])
  XLOOKUP(value, lookup, return, [if_not_found], [match_mode], [search_mode])
  INDEX(range, row, [column])
  MATCH(value, range, [type])          0 exact; 1 largest ≤ (default); -1 smallest ≥
  SUMIF(range, criteria, [sum_range])
  SUMIFS(sum_range, range1, crit1, ...)
  COUNTIF(range, criteria)
  COUNTIFS(range1, crit1, ...)

  Criteria may be a value, ">10", "<>x", or a wildcard (* and ?; ~ escapes).

DATE
  TODAY()
  NOW()
  DATE(year, month, day)
  YEAR(date)  MONTH(date)  DAY(date)
  WEEKDAY(date, [type])     1: Sun=1 (default); 2: Mon=1; 3: Mon=0
  DATEDIF(start, end, unit) Y, M, D, YM, MD, YD
  EDATE(start, months)
  Dates are YYYY-MM-DD (same as TODAY). Serial numbers from 1 March 1900
  match Excel; earlier serials may be a day off (Excel's 1900 leap-year bug
  is not reproduced).

SHEET REFS (SH8)
  Sheet2!A1           a cell on another sheet
  Sheet2!A1:B5        a range on another sheet
  'My Sheet'!A1       a name with a space (or other punctuation) must be quoted
  Renaming a sheet updates these; deleting it turns them into #REF!

ABSOLUTE REFS ($)

  $A1       column fixed

GRID
  Columns A–Z (26)

ERRORS
  #DIV/0!   division by zero
  #NUM!     number too large / not a number (e.g. 10^400, SQRT(-1))
  #REF!     reference outside the sheet (e.g. A150), a deleted cell, or a missing sheet
  #LOOP!    circular reference (A1=B1, B1=A1)
  #N/A      lookup not found, or IFS/SWITCH with no match
  #VALUE!   wrong type of argument (e.g. FIND with no match, a bad date)
  #ERR      formula not understood

FORMULA HINT / PICKER (SH9)
  While typing a formula, a hint under the formula bar shows the current
  function and argument. The fx button opens a searchable function list.

SHORTCUTS (Ctrl or Cmd)

  C / X / V copy, cut, paste (tab-separated)
  D fill down · R fill right
  F find · H find and replace
  Shift+arrows / drag / Shift+click   select a block
  Delete / Backspace                  clear selection
  F2 / double-click                   edit, keeping the text
