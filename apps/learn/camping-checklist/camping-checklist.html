Leave No Trace & India trip notes

    Adapt to the place

    Monsoon: Check flood and landslide alerts. Avoid swollen streams, gullies and exposed ridges during lightning. Use dry liners; inspect for leeches and mosquito bites.

    Himalayan treks: Cold gear alone does not make high altitude safe. Plan gradual ascent and acclimatisation with trained local guidance. Do not ascend with altitude-illness symptoms; descend and get medical help if worsening. Snow, glaciers and avalanche terrain require specialist skills and equipment.

    Hot plains: Start early, use shade and avoid midday exertion. Water needs vary with heat and effort. Do not force excessive drinking. Stop and seek help for confusion, collapse or heat illness.

    Water: Treat natural sources even if clear. Boil at a rolling boil for 1 minute, or 3 minutes above about 2,000 m; alternatively filter then disinfect according to product instructions. Treatment does not remove chemical pollution or salt. Avoid suspect sources.

    Beach: Camp only where permitted, well above high tide. Check tides, currents and weather; do not rely on an app checklist for swimming safety.

    Learning references (internet needed): Leave No Trace: seven principles · NPS: backcountry health · NPS: hot-weather hiking · India ERSS 112. This is a plain-language educational summary, not a substitute for local rules or professional advice.
