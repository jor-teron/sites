title: Nosebleed
icon: 🩸
summary: Lean forward and pinch the soft part for 10 minutes.
call: Get urgent medical help if the bleeding has not stopped after 20 minutes, is very heavy, follows a head injury, or you feel faint or have trouble breathing.

[basics]
1. Sit down and lean forward a little. Do not tip your head back.
2. Pinch the soft part of the nose, just below the bony bridge, with your thumb and finger. Breathe through your mouth.
3. Keep pinching for 10 full minutes without letting go to check. Use a timer.
4. Spit out any blood that reaches your mouth instead of swallowing it.
5. When it has stopped, rest. Do not blow, pick or rub your nose, and avoid hot drinks and hard exercise for the rest of the day.

[expert]
- Why lean forward: with the head back, blood runs down the throat. It can make you cough, gag or feel sick, and you cannot tell how much you are losing.
- If it starts again, blow your nose gently once to clear clots, then pinch again for another 10 minutes.
- Most nosebleeds come from the front of the nose and are harmless. Frequent nosebleeds, or nosebleeds while taking blood thinners, should be discussed with a doctor.
- Common causes: dry air, colds, picking, a knock to the nose. A pharmacist can suggest ways to keep the inside of the nose moist.
