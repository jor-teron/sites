title: Fainting
icon: 💫
summary: Lay them down, raise the legs, check breathing.
call: Call emergency services if the person does not wake up within 1 minute, is not breathing normally, was hurt in the fall, is pregnant, has chest pain, or faints again.

[basics]
1. If you feel faint, sit or lie down straight away. If someone faints, check the area is safe and ease their fall if you can.
2. Lay them on their back and raise their legs about 30 cm (12 inches) above the heart, unless you suspect an injury.
3. Loosen tight clothing such as a collar or belt, and make sure they have fresh air.
4. Check their breathing. If they are not breathing normally, call emergency services and start CPR.
5. When they wake up, keep them lying down for a few minutes, then help them sit up slowly. Give water only when they are fully alert.

[expert]
- Common causes: standing up quickly, heat, not enough water or food, pain, and strong emotions. Warning signs are feeling dizzy, pale, sweaty, or seeing spots.
- Do not slap, shake or splash the person, and do not sit them upright while they are unresponsive.
- If they are unresponsive but breathing normally, or they vomit, put them in the recovery position (on their side) and call emergency services.
- See a doctor after a first faint, or if it came with chest pain, a racing heartbeat, an injury, no warning, or happened during exercise.
