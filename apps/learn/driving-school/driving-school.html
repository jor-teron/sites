How to drive

This is a conceptual practice simulator, not professional instruction. Never use it while driving. Use a qualified instructor for a real car; rules and test criteria vary by location.

  1. Adjust settings: India uses LEFT-hand traffic and a RIGHT-hand-drive seat. Choose Manual or Automatic. You are looking down on the car; its headlights point forward.
  2. Manual start: handbrake on; hold Clutch (C), start engine (Enter), select first (E), check Mirrors (M) and all around. Release handbrake (H). Add gentle accelerator (W) and release clutch. The clutch gauge shows the bite point. A stall needs brake, clutch down, neutral, restart.
  3. Accelerate in a straight line, ease off W, clutch down, select the next gear, release clutch. First gear is for moving off. Look ahead through turns and reduce speed before steering.
  4. To stop: release accelerator, apply brake (S/Space) progressively. Hold clutch near walking speed so the engine does not stall. At rest select neutral and apply handbrake.
  5. For reverse: stop completely, clutch down, Q to R, check behind and blind spots. Use light throttle. Steering reverses its effect on the direction of the nose while backing.
  6. Automatic: choose a forward gear with Gear + (E) or R via Gear − (Q) from N. No clutch needed. It shifts forward gears only. Always stop before changing direction.
  7. Touch: drag the steering wheel sideways; release to centre. Hold accelerator/brake/clutch. Gear and signal buttons latch. Keyboard arrows steer/accelerate/brake; Z/X signals, B horn. Indicators do not cancel automatically.
  8. Follow the blue rings in order. Move-off and braking lessons finish in a blue stop box. Parking needs full alignment inside the bay, neutral and handbrake. Retry resets only the current attempt; best scores stay saved.
  9. Town limit is 30 km/h. Stop fully at STOP. Red means stop before the line. Amber: do not begin entering. Give way to pedestrians and traffic already on the roundabout. Signal BEFORE turning, cancel after.
  10. Mock test: follow the whole route then parallel park. End test shows an incomplete report. Any serious fault fails the practice test; at most 10 minor faults pass. These are practice thresholds, not official licence rules.
