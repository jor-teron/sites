How to drive

This is a conceptual practice simulator, not professional instruction. Never use it while driving. Use a qualified instructor for a real car; rules and test criteria vary by location.

  1. Adjust settings: India uses LEFT-hand traffic and a RIGHT-hand-drive seat. Manual is the default; Automatic is in Settings. You sit in the driver's seat (on the right in India) and look out through the windscreen. A mini-map can be turned on in Settings (off by default). The edge-glow fault alert is on by default and can be turned off there.
  2. Manual start: handbrake on; hold Clutch (C), start or stop the engine (Enter), select first (E), check Mirrors (M) and all around. Release handbrake (H). Add gentle accelerator (W) and release clutch. The clutch gauge shows the bite point. A stall needs brake, clutch down, neutral, restart.
  3. Accelerate in a straight line, ease off W, clutch down, select the next gear, release clutch. First gear is for moving off. Look ahead through turns and reduce speed before steering.
  4. To stop: release accelerator, apply brake (S/Space) progressively. Hold clutch near walking speed so the engine does not stall. At rest select neutral and apply handbrake.
  5. For reverse: stop completely, clutch down, Q to R, check behind and blind spots. Use light throttle. Steering reverses its effect on the direction of the nose while backing.
  6. Automatic: choose a forward gear with Gear + (E) or R via Gear − (Q) from N. No clutch needed. It shifts forward gears only. Always stop before changing direction.
  7. Touch: drag the steering wheel round; let go and it straightens. Tap the middle of the wheel for a short horn beep, or hold it (or hold B) to keep the horn on until you let go. Tap a slot on the gear lever to change gear. Hold accelerator/brake/clutch. Gear and signal buttons latch. Keyboard arrows steer/accelerate/brake; Z/X signals. Indicators do not cancel automatically.
  8. Drive through the blue arches (rings) in order; the next one glows. Move-off and braking lessons finish in a blue stop box. Parking needs full alignment inside the bay, neutral and handbrake. The live score starts at 100% and is shown on the dashboard (green 80 or above, amber 50–79, red below 50). A minor fault takes off 5, a serious fault 25; clean driving wins 1 back for every 200 m without a fault. Retry resets only the current attempt; best scores stay saved.
  9. Town limit is 30 km/h. Stop fully at STOP. Red means stop before the line. Amber: do not begin entering. Give way to pedestrians and traffic already on the roundabout. Signal BEFORE turning, cancel after.
  10. Open road: Endless drive keeps making new road ahead of you; Practice loop is a closed loop you finish by driving one lap. Type a route number: the same number always gives the same road. Roads change type (city 40, highway 80, ghat 30, village 25 km/h). Keep left and on the tarmac, slow to 20 km/h for speed breakers, stop for people on zebra crossings, and give way at roundabouts.
  11. Mock test: follow the whole route then parallel park. End test shows an incomplete report. Any serious fault fails the practice test; at most 10 minor faults pass. These are practice thresholds, not official licence rules.
