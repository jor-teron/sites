title: Fractions, Decimals and Percent
icon: ➗
summary: See how fractions, decimals and percentages are the same idea, and use them for discounts and tips.

[basics]
1. A fraction is part of a whole. In 3/4, the bottom number (4) is how many equal parts, and the top number (3) is how many you have.
2. A decimal shows parts of ten, hundred and so on: 0.5 is 5 tenths, 0.25 is 25 hundredths.
3. Percent means "out of 100": 25% means 25 out of 100, which is 25/100 = 0.25 = 1/4.
4. Learn the common ones: 1/2 = 0.5 = 50%, 1/4 = 0.25 = 25%, 3/4 = 0.75 = 75%, 1/5 = 0.2 = 20%, 1/10 = 0.1 = 10%.
5. Fraction to decimal: divide top by bottom. 3/8 = 3 ÷ 8 = 0.375.
6. Decimal to percent: multiply by 100. 0.375 = 37.5%. Percent to decimal: divide by 100. 8% = 0.08.
7. 10% of a number: move the decimal point one place left. 10% of ₹450 = ₹45.
8. Build other percentages from 10%: 5% is half of 10%, 20% is double. 20% of ₹450 = ₹90; 5% of ₹450 = ₹22.50.
9. Discount: a ₹1,200 shirt at 25% off. 25% of 1,200 = 300, so you pay ₹1,200 − ₹300 = ₹900.
10. Quick way: paying after 25% off means paying 75%. 0.75 × 1,200 = ₹900.
11. Tip: a ₹840 restaurant bill with a 10% tip. 10% = ₹84, so the total is ₹924.
12. Simplify fractions by dividing top and bottom by the same number: 6/8 = 3/4 (both divided by 2).

[expert]
- Percentage change = (new − old) ÷ old × 100. Rent going from ₹8,000 to ₹9,000 is a 1,000 ÷ 8,000 × 100 = 12.5% rise.
- A fall of 20% followed by a rise of 20% does not get you back. ₹100 → ₹80 → ₹96. The second 20% is of a smaller number.
- Two discounts stack by multiplying, not adding. 20% off then a further 10% off is 0.8 × 0.9 = 0.72 of the price: 28% off, not 30%.
- Reverse percentage: a price is ₹1,180 including 18% GST. The price before GST is 1,180 ÷ 1.18 = ₹1,000, not 1,180 − 18%.
- x% of y equals y% of x: 8% of 50 is the same as 50% of 8, which is 4.
- Adding fractions needs the same bottom: 1/3 + 1/4 = 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12.
- Multiplying fractions: top × top over bottom × bottom. 2/3 × 3/5 = 6/15 = 2/5. Dividing: flip the second and multiply, so 1/2 ÷ 1/4 = 1/2 × 4 = 2.
- Compare fractions by cross-multiplying: is 5/8 bigger than 3/5? 5 × 5 = 25 and 3 × 8 = 24, so yes, 5/8 is bigger.
- Recurring decimals: 1/3 = 0.333…, 2/3 = 0.666…, 1/9 = 0.111…, 1/7 = 0.142857 142857…
- Percentage points vs percent: an interest rate going from 6% to 8% is a rise of 2 percentage points, but a 33.3% rise in the rate itself.
- Splitting in a ratio: share ₹1,500 in the ratio 2 : 3. Total parts = 5, one part = ₹300, so the shares are ₹600 and ₹900.
- Tip on a split bill: a ₹2,400 bill plus 10% tip shared by 4 people is 2,640 ÷ 4 = ₹660 each.
