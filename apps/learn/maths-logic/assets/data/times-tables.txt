title: Times Tables and Tricks
icon: ✖️
summary: Shortcuts for the 9s and 11s, doubling and halving, and other tricks that make multiplying easy.

[basics]
1. Multiplying is repeated adding: 4 × 3 means 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12. The order does not matter, so 4 × 3 = 3 × 4.
2. You only need to learn about half the table, because 7 × 8 is the same as 8 × 7 (both 56).
3. Times 10: put a zero on the end. 23 × 10 = 230. Times 100: put two zeros. 23 × 100 = 2,300.
4. Times 5: multiply by 10, then halve. 36 × 5 = 360 ÷ 2 = 180.
5. 9s trick: 9 × 7 = 70 − 7 = 63. For 9 × any number, multiply by 10 and take the number away once.
6. 9s check: the digits of 9 × 1 to 9 × 10 always add up to 9: 18, 27, 36, 45, 54, 63, 72, 81, 90.
7. 9s on your fingers: hold up 10 fingers and fold down finger number 4 for 9 × 4. 3 fingers stand on the left and 6 on the right: 36.
8. 11s for one digit: repeat the digit. 11 × 6 = 66, 11 × 8 = 88.
9. 11s for two digits: split the digits and put their sum in the middle. 11 × 34: 3 _ 4, 3 + 4 = 7, so 374.
10. Doubling is times 2: double 37 = 30 + 30 + 7 + 7 = 74. Times 4 is double, then double again. Times 8 is double three times.
11. Halving is ÷ 2: half of 86 = half of 80 + half of 6 = 40 + 3 = 43.
12. Double one number and halve the other, and the answer stays the same: 16 × 25 = 8 × 50 = 4 × 100 = 400.

[expert]
- 11s with a carry: if the middle sum is 10 or more, carry 1 to the left. 11 × 78: 7 + 8 = 15, so 7+1, 5, 8 = 858.
- 11 × a three-digit number: 11 × 243 = 2, (2 + 4), (4 + 3), 3 = 2,673.
- Times 9 for big numbers: 9 × 46 = 460 − 46 = 414. Times 99: 99 × 46 = 4,600 − 46 = 4,554.
- Times 25: multiply by 100, then divide by 4. 48 × 25 = 4,800 ÷ 4 = 1,200.
- Squares ending in 5: take the tens digit, multiply it by the next number, then write 25. 35² = (3 × 4) then 25 = 1,225; 85² = (8 × 9) then 25 = 7,225.
- Split a number to multiply (the distributive law): 7 × 48 = 7 × 50 − 7 × 2 = 350 − 14 = 336.
- Near 100: 98 × 97. Each is 2 and 3 below 100. Take 98 − 3 = 95, and 2 × 3 = 06, so 9,506.
- Difference of squares: 21 × 19 = 20² − 1² = 400 − 1 = 399. Works whenever two numbers sit equally either side of an easy number.
- Times 12: times 10 plus times 2. 12 × 17 = 170 + 34 = 204.
- Halving odd numbers gives a half: half of 75 = 37.5. Halve a price: ₹75 split between 2 people is ₹37.50 each.
- Check a product with digit sums (casting out nines): 23 × 47 = 1,081. Digit sums: 23 → 5 and 47 → 11 → 2, so 5 × 2 = 10 → 1. The answer 1,081 → 1 + 0 + 8 + 1 = 10 → 1. They match, so it is probably right.
- Even × anything is even; odd × odd is odd. So 7 × 13 cannot be 90.
