title: Shapes and Measuring
icon: 📐
summary: Measure length, area and volume in metric units, and convert between them.

[basics]
1. Length units: 10 mm = 1 cm, 100 cm = 1 m, 1,000 m = 1 km.
2. Mass units: 1,000 g = 1 kg, 1,000 kg = 1 tonne. Volume units: 1,000 mL = 1 L.
3. Perimeter is the distance all the way round a shape. Add up all the sides.
4. Rectangle perimeter = 2 × (length + width). A room 5 m by 4 m has a perimeter of 2 × 9 = 18 m.
5. Square perimeter = 4 × side. A square garden with 6 m sides needs 24 m of fencing.
6. Area is the space inside a shape, measured in square units like cm² or m².
7. Rectangle area = length × width. The 5 m by 4 m room has an area of 20 m².
8. Triangle area = ½ × base × height. Base 6 cm, height 4 cm: area = 12 cm².
9. Circle: the distance round (circumference) = 2 × π × radius, and area = π × radius². π is about 3.14 (or 22/7).
10. A circle with radius 7 cm has circumference about 44 cm and area about 154 cm² (using 22/7).
11. Volume of a box = length × width × height. A tank 2 m × 1 m × 1 m holds 2 m³.
12. Real use: flooring a 20 m² room with tiles at ₹45 per m² costs 20 × 45 = ₹900, plus extra for cut pieces.

[expert]
- Area units scale by the square: 1 m = 100 cm, so 1 m² = 100 × 100 = 10,000 cm². Do not multiply by 100 only.
- Volume units scale by the cube: 1 m³ = 100 × 100 × 100 = 10,00,000 cm³ = 1,000 litres.
- 1 litre = 1,000 cm³ (a 10 cm cube). 1 mL = 1 cm³. A tank 2 m × 1 m × 1 m holds 2,000 litres.
- 1 hectare = 10,000 m² (a 100 m by 100 m square). 1 km² = 100 hectares.
- Doubling every side of a shape makes its area 4 times bigger and a solid's volume 8 times bigger.
- Pythagoras for right-angled triangles: a² + b² = c². Sides 3 m and 4 m give a long side of 5 m. Builders use 3-4-5 to check a square corner.
- Cylinder volume = π × r² × h. A water drum with radius 0.3 m and height 1 m holds about 3.14 × 0.09 × 1 ≈ 0.28 m³ ≈ 280 litres.
- Paint: walls 3 m high round a 5 m × 4 m room have area 18 × 3 = 54 m². At 10 m² per litre, you need about 5.4 litres per coat, minus doors and windows.
- Speed = distance ÷ time. 120 km in 2 hours is 60 km/h. To change km/h to m/s, multiply by 5/18: 72 km/h = 20 m/s.
- Temperature: °F = °C × 9/5 + 32. 37 °C (body) = 98.6 °F. 100 °C = 212 °F.
- Scale maps: on a 1 : 50,000 map, 1 cm stands for 50,000 cm = 500 m, so 4 cm is 2 km.
- Angles: a full turn is 360°, a right angle 90°. The angles in any triangle add to 180°, and in any four-sided shape to 360°.
- Shapes that tile a floor with no gaps include squares, rectangles, equal triangles and regular hexagons.
