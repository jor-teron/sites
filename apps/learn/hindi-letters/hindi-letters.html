There is no timer. Take your time.

How to use

  1. Letters: tap any card for its sound hint and picture word. Mark it learned when you recognise it.
  2. Trace the faint glyph with a finger, pen or mouse. Number 1: curves and stems. Number 2: the top line (शिरोरेखा), left to right, last. The arrows are broad phases, not exact pen strokes. Follow the actual faint shape. Some letters have little or no continuous headline.
  3. Trace stars combine coverage and accuracy. Filling the entire box does not earn full stars. Clear starts a new attempt; your best result stays saved.
  4. Matras: choose a consonant; tap a form for its explanation. The इ sign is encoded after the consonant even though you see it on the left. अं and अः are nasal/breath signs.
  5. Joined letters: a halant removes the inherent अ sound. Learn half forms, then क्ष त्र ज्ञ श्र प्र द्ध. Different fonts can draw conjuncts differently.
  6. Numbers teaches १–१०. Words has 200 entries in eight themes. Search by Hindi or English. Emoji are approximate memory cues; read the meaning too.
  7. Games: see or hear a letter and choose; match a picture and meaning to a word; or build a word from letter-group tiles. The letter groups (aksharas) keep matras and conjuncts attached to their base. Listen & pick shows transliteration if speech is unavailable or off. Hear is manual so you can take your time.
  8. Each game has 10 questions. 4, 7 or 9 correct earns 1, 2 or 3 stars. Best game stars and trace stars are saved. Learn a letter, earn trace stars or finish a game to count an active day. Practise on consecutive local-calendar days to grow your streak.
  9. Pause / quit (or Escape) stops practice and speech. A hidden tab pauses an active game. Quitting an unfinished game does not award stars. There are no timers or penalties.
  10. Settings starts at Auto (System / Hub). A Hindi device voice enables spoken Hindi; pronunciation quality varies by device. Audio can be switched off; Sound off mutes speech and effects. Progress stays only on this browser; clearing its site data erases it.

A beginner recognition and shape-practice aid, not a substitute for a Hindi teacher. The chart uses the traditional 13 vowel/sign entries and 33 basic consonants plus 3 conjuncts. Additional nukta letters such as ड़ and ढ़ appear in words but are outside the basic chart. Roman hints are approximate.
