Water Kingdom - Children's Learning App
Full Book – Chapters 1 to 7

Chapters:
1. Meet Droppy
2. Droppy’s Journey (Water Cycle)
3. Friends of the Water
4. Water’s Three Forms
5. Why Water Matters
6. Be a Water Hero
7. Bedtime Story

How to use:
1. Unzip.
2. Open index.html in a browser.
3. Choose any chapter from the menu.
4. Use Prev / Next to turn pages.

All chapters are modular under modules/chapter-XX/.
