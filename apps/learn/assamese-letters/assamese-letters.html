How to use

  1. Letters: tap any card for its Assamese sound, a hint and a picture word. Mark it learned when you recognise it.
  2. Labels: the tile shows a simple roman spelling. Where Assamese says a letter very differently the sound follows a slash: শ ষ স = śa/xa ṣa/xa sa/xa (the Assamese x, like ch in Scottish loch), চ ছ = ca/sa cha/sa, জ ঝ য = ja/za jha/za ya/za. The letter card also shows the Assamese sound.
  3. Trace the faint glyph with a finger, pen or mouse. Number 1: curves and stems. Number 2: the top line (মাত্ৰা), left to right, last. The arrows are broad phases, not exact pen strokes. Follow the actual faint shape. Some letters such as এ ঐ ও ঔ have little or no top line.
  4. Trace stars combine coverage and accuracy, shown live in the Covered and On the guide bars. Filling the entire box does not earn full stars. Clear starts a new attempt; your best result stays saved.
  5. Matras: choose a consonant; tap a form for its explanation. ি ে ৈ are typed after the consonant even though you see them on the left; ো ৌ wrap around it. ং ঃ ঁ are nasal and breath signs.
  6. Joined letters: a hasanta (্) removes the inherent অ. Learn ক্ষ জ্ঞ ন্ত স্ত ক্ত ঙ্ক ন্দ ম্প ত্ৰ শ্ৰ plus ৰেফ and য-ফলা. Different fonts can draw joined letters differently.
  7. Numbers teaches ০–১০. Words has 143 entries in eight themes. Search by Assamese or English. Emoji are approximate memory cues; read the meaning too.
  8. Games: see or hear a letter and choose; match a picture and meaning to a word; or build a word from letter-group tiles. Letter groups keep vowel signs and joined letters attached to their base. Listen & pick uses one letter per Assamese sound (for example স but not শ or ষ) and shows the roman label if speech is unavailable or off. Hear is manual so you can take your time.
  9. Each game has 10 questions. 4, 7 or 9 correct earns 1, 2 or 3 stars. Best game stars and trace stars are saved. Learn a letter, earn trace stars or finish a game to count an active day. Practise on consecutive local-calendar days to grow your streak.
  10. Pause / quit (or Escape) stops practice and speech. A hidden tab pauses an active game. Quitting an unfinished game does not award stars. There are no timers or penalties.
  11. The theme follows your system or Hub automatically. Sound is on by default; the Sound button on the title bar turns speech and effects on or off. Speech uses an Assamese device voice if there is one, else a Bengali voice (same script, some sounds differ), else a Hindi voice (may not read Assamese well). Progress stays only on this browser; clearing its site data erases it.
