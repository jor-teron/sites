Dictionary (apps/learn/dictionary) - data licence and credits
=============================================================

The word data in data/ is converted from Open English WordNet 2025
(https://en-word.net/ , https://github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet)
by tools/build_data.py. Changes made: entries regrouped by word, split into
small JSON files, examples cut to 2 and synonyms to 6 per sense, other
relations left out. Meanings keep the order Open English WordNet lists
them in; word families and phrases are ordered by how often they appear
in its own definitions and examples.
