title: Everyday Words
icon: 💬
summary: About 30 common words with example sentences.

[basics]
1. Greetings: hello ("Hello, Sam."), goodbye ("Goodbye, see you soon."), please ("Please sit down."), thanks ("Thanks for your help."), sorry ("Sorry, I am late.").
2. Family: mother ("My mother is kind."), father ("My father works here."), sister ("I have one sister."), brother ("My brother is tall."), friend ("She is my friend.").
3. Food and drink: bread ("I eat bread."), rice ("We cook rice."), apple ("The apple is sweet."), water ("I drink water."), milk ("Milk is white.").
4. Colours: blue ("The sky is blue."), green ("The grass is green."), yellow ("A banana is yellow."), black ("My bag is black."), white ("The cup is white.").
5. Numbers: one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten ("I have two books.").
6. Use a word three times in the same day, in a sentence you make yourself, and it will stay in your memory.

[expert]
- Learn words in groups (family, food, colours) rather than in a long random list. Related words help each other stick.
- "Please" and "thank you" make a request polite: "Can I have some water, please?"
- Numbers continue: eleven, twelve, thirteen, and so on. Tens end in -ty: twenty, thirty, forty.
- Many nouns take "a" or "an" in front: "a book", "an apple". Use "an" before a vowel sound.
- Keep a small notebook. Write the word, a short sentence, and a picture or a word in your own language.
