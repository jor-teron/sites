title: Common Phrases
icon: 👋
summary: Greetings, polite words and asking for help.

[basics]
1. Greet people: "Hello" or "Hi" at any time. "Good morning", "Good afternoon" and "Good evening" suit the time of day.
2. Say thank you: "Thank you" or "Thanks". The reply is "You're welcome."
3. Ask politely with please: "Can I have some water, please?"
4. Get attention or pass by with "Excuse me": "Excuse me, where is the station?"
5. Say "Sorry" when you make a mistake. The reply is "That's okay" or "No problem."
6. Ask for help: "Can you help me, please?" or "I don't understand. Can you say that again?"
7. When you leave, say "Goodbye", "Bye" or "See you later."

[expert]
- "Excuse me" is for getting attention. "Sorry" is for an apology. Do not swap them.
- "How do you do?" is very formal. In daily life, "Nice to meet you" is more usual.
- "Pardon?" or "Sorry?" asks someone to repeat something, and it is polite.
- "Could you..." is a little softer than "Can you...", so use it with people you do not know.
- Say "Good night" only when someone is going to bed or you are leaving late at night.
