title: Questions and Answers
icon: 🗣️
summary: Who, what, where, when, why and how.

[basics]
1. Who asks about a person. "Who is that?" "That is my teacher."
2. What asks about a thing. "What is this?" "It is a pen."
3. Where asks about a place. "Where is the bank?" "It is near the station."
4. When asks about time. "When is the class?" "It is at nine o'clock."
5. Why asks for a reason, and the answer often starts with "because". "Why are you happy?" "Because it is Friday."
6. How asks about the way or the state of something. "How are you?" "I am fine, thank you."

[expert]
- For yes/no questions, put the verb first: "Are you ready?" "Yes, I am." or "No, I am not."
- With other verbs, use do or does: "Do you like tea?" "Does she live here?"
- "How much" asks about a price or an amount that cannot be counted. "How many" asks about things you can count.
- A question ends with a question mark, and the voice usually rises at the end of a yes/no question.
- Short answers sound natural: "Yes, I do." is friendlier than only "Yes."
