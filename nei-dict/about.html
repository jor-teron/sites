About nei-dict

nei-dict is a simple browser dictionary for Northeast Indian languages. Look up words in English, Karbi, Dimasa, Hmar, Meeteilon, Mizo, Nagamese, Khasi, Kok-Borok, Singpho, and Nepali, with light search that still helps when a spelling is slightly off.
