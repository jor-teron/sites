What the Hub is

The Hub started as one page, hub.html, that opened the site's apps in windows. It grew into the base that everything else runs on: settings, themes, linking devices, sharing files and keeping things in sync. It is a website, so there is nothing to install, and it also works offline once it has been opened.

How it is built

  • hub/ is the core. It holds the parts every mode shares: your settings and profile, day and night themes, keys, device linking, the Mesh, sync, and updates.
  • desktop/ and phone/ are the screens on top. They are two different ways to look at the same Hub. Your apps, files and links stay the same whichever one you use.
  • This page (hub/index.html) is just a normal webpage about the Hub.

Two modes

  • Desktop: windows, a taskbar and right-click menus. Best with a mouse and keyboard.
  • Phone | Touch: one app at a time, full screen, with swipes. Best for phones and touch tablets.

The first page of the site shows the two cards and suggests one for your screen. Your pick is saved on this device (localStorage key sites-hub.mode), so next time you go straight there.

  • index.html?os=desktop or ?os=phone opens that mode and saves it.
  • index.html?choose clears the saved choice and shows the cards again.
  • You can also switch from inside each mode, after a confirm.

Connect codes and the Mesh

  • A Connect code is 5 digits. Devices with the same code can find each other, chat and share files.
  • 00000 is the public Mesh lobby: anyone on that code can see you.
  • Any other code is a private mesh: only devices that know the code join it.

System apps

The Hub comes with its own apps under apps/system/, such as Settings, Files (with network drives), the Viewer and the App Hub that lists every other app. Other apps and games live in their own folders and open inside the Hub.
